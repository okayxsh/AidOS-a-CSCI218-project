## Scope

Landing page `/` (Persuade) and dashboard `/dashboard` (Operate), one visual world. Audience: CSCI218 markers and general visitors, weighted equally. Action: Launch Dashboard.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cinematic night view of a fictional relief landscape that introduces a calm, explainable allocation tool. It refuses the split hero (copy beside a screenshot) and the feature-card wall.

OWN-WORLD: Night navy sky (#04070d to #16406a at the horizon), faint aurora bands in teal and blue at low opacity, and a 56px grid fading toward the edges. The ridge silhouette holds a depot, shelter tents, a water tower and sparse zone lights joined by dashed supply routes. Mona Sans handles UI and display. Instrument Serif italic is used only for the accent line. White and near-black pill buttons. Surfaces are #0c1626 with hairlines at rgba(170,205,245,.1). Teal #3fd0c9 marks stock, amber #f5b042 marks shortfall, and red appears only for critical.

STORY: The visitor learns that zones compete for scarce stock, sees how AidOS assesses, allocates and adapts, sees which parts are built and which are planned, and launches the dashboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rounded frame inset 20px. Header with the wordmark at left, a centred nav, and Launch Dashboard at right. A centred two-line headline at 72px, with the second line in serif italic at 86px. Subcopy under 620px wide. Pills: Launch Dashboard (white, primary) and Explore how it works. A one-line academic note. The dashboard window sits from y≈560 to the frame bottom, in front of the ridge.

FORM: Aurora Ridge, refined by the user from their reference image; grounded direction, user-pinned. seed:36ebfad10251a12f

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

No excess glow, gradients or animation. Every dashboard number comes from the backend. Planned stages are labelled as planned.
