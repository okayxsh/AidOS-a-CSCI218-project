# Individual contribution and reflection statement: template

**For each student to complete individually and truthfully.** The guidelines (section 6.2) require a 1–2 page statement, submitted separately by each student. This template gives structure only. Nothing in it may be filled with invented work, and every claim must be checkable against the repository, the report or the contribution log. Claims contradicted by the repository fall into the lowest band.

Delete the guidance in *italics* before submitting. Keep it to 1–2 pages.
